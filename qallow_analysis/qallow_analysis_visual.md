# Visual Design Specification

## Visual Identity

- The overall design aesthetic follows a 'Quantum Blueprint' concept, merging the precision of architectural schematics with the atmospheric depth of deep space. The visual language is minimalist and technical, treating each slide as a viewport into a sophisticated quantum computing interface. The core idea is to present complex analysis with absolute clarity and authority, using a dark, focused environment to make data and insights the heroes.

- The visual narrative evokes the feeling of examining a classified research document on a high-resolution display within a low-light laboratory. A deep, dark background minimizes visual noise, creating a canvas where information — composed in crisp, light text and accentuated by a single, potent color — takes center stage. The accent color is used sparingly, like a laser pointer, to guide attention to critical findings, code snippets, and improvement vectors, creating a rhythm of discovery and focus.

## Color Scheme

- **Background Color**: #1A1D21
- **Primary Color**: #EAEAEA
- **Secondary Color**: #888B8D
- **Accent Color**: #A8D8EA
